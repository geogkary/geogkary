# How you doin' 👋

Let's clear the air real quick. I'm not an AI specialist.

I'm not a transformation expert, consultant or content guru with magic prompts. 

I don't barge in with third-party services, blackbox solutions, retainers or technical audits about which workflow automations to implement and where to introduce autonomous processes.

Instead, I work with SMEs implementing AI on their own.

I provide tools, resources, frameworks and hands-on bootcamps as part of a delivery management process grounded in software and product development principles. A process crafted across:

- 20+ years of cross-functional experience.
- 100+ public/private sector projects, incl. leading the launch of major initiatives.
- 2M+ in budgets handled, incl. scaling an agency to 7 figures and securing a 6-figure VC investment for a startup.
- 10+ industries in Europe, Australia and the USA.

You know, from a time before AI.
